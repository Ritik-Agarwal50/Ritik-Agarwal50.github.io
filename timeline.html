Timeline & Research Log

A chronological log of my path: learning by building software, competitive hackathons, the shift into smart contract vulnerability research, and current inquiries into zero-knowledge cryptography.

2026 — Present

Zero-Knowledge Cryptography & Systems Research

Expanding research focus from application-layer smart contract auditing into the mathematical machinery of zero-knowledge proofs. Authoring Intro to ZK Math, an open working guide explaining polynomial commitment schemes (KZG), the Schwartz-Zippel lemma, arithmetization, and Rank-1 Constraint Systems (R1CS).

Concurrently developing an independent research project exploring decentralized, serverless communication protocols that eliminate centralized metadata collection.

2025 — 2026

Software Supply-Chain Security with Shim (Go)

Co-founded and engineered Shim — a Go-based security proxy for package managers (npm, pnpm, yarn).

Shim intercepts package install lifecycle hooks, runs local rule evaluation against static threat models, and cross-references vulnerability feeds to prevent supply-chain compromises before arbitrary post-install scripts execute. Features an IPC background daemon, minimal latency overhead, and an SQLite cache.

2025

Full-Time Blockchain Security & Cantina Podium Finish

In January 2025, transitioned into smart contract security research full-time. Focused on deep manual code inspection, Foundry invariant test suites, and dissecting subtle EVM execution edge cases.

Achieved a podium finish by placing 3rd out of 723 security researchers in Cantina's Kuru Protocol security review, identifying 4 high/medium severity vulnerabilities in order-book DEX and multi-asset vault contracts. Over the course of the year, reported 18 validated security findings across 8 public audit competitions, alongside private NDA reviews for institutional protocols.

2024

The Catalyst: From Building Protocols to Breaking Them

Completed a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) with 84% and commenced MCA studies at Jain University.

In mid-2024, seeing how single architectural oversights and subtle state-transition flaws could compromise entire decentralized systems, I shifted my focus entirely from standard software engineering to offensive smart contract security, invariant testing, and EVM internals.

The Early Crucible: My initial 15–20 vulnerability submissions to competitive audit platforms were rejected as invalid or deemed out of scope. Rather than discouraging me, this brutal feedback loop taught me the discipline of formal security research: every submission must be accompanied by a deterministic, mathematically verifiable proof-of-concept demonstrating real economic impact.
2023

Led technical architecture across hackathons, writing Solidity smart contracts and integrating complex decentralized backend services:

  • Cyber Shadez (1st Place): Won 1st place for Sandesh, a decentralized encrypted messaging protocol.
  • DU Hacks (Polygon Track Winner): Built Save Tiger, an ERC-721 conservation tracking and fundraising DApp using Next.js and ethers.js.
  • Cyber Shadez (2nd Place): Architected an ERC-4337 Account Abstraction wallet project with Alchemy bundlers, Next.js, Prisma, and Supabase.
2022

First Smart Contract Deployments & On-Chain Mechanics

Studied the Ethereum Yellow Paper, Bitcoin whitepaper, and EVM mechanics. Built and deployed full-stack NFT minting contracts to the Ethereum Ropsten testnet, learning the intricacies of gas limits, state variables, and transaction lifecycles.

2020 — 2021

Entered university (GLS University) and explored web development, systems programming, and machine learning theory before discovering distributed consensus and decentralized protocols.
